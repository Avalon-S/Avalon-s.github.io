  1. 02

    Research Intern

    Trento, Trentino-Alto Adige, Italy · On-site
    Supervisors: Yiming Wang, Elisa Ricci

    • Proposed Active Instance Verification and built its first benchmark, PInVerify. Navigation benchmarks count arrival as success, yet an agent can stop in front of the wrong instance of the right category. Formalized this stage as a finite-horizon decision process and built 3,000 evaluation episodes over 18 object categories in photorealistic 3D scenes, with trap views and unreachable sectors in the navigation topology.
    • Designed the evaluation protocol and failure diagnostics. Positive, same-category and cross-category pairs are balanced 1:1:1 to separate calibration bias from category recognition; beyond accuracy, the protocol reports steps to decision and navigation failure rate, and the training pool is disjoint from the test set. Judging differences by 95% confidence intervals located the main bottleneck in detection (+3.1 pp with ground-truth boxes) and found no reliable difference among three next-best-view strategies.
    • Built two baseline tracks, training-free and trained. The training-free track is a modular pipeline (attribute decomposition, multi-view tracking and fusion, three next-best-view strategies), evaluated on Qwen3-VL-4B / 8B, SenseNova-SI-InternVL3-8B, CLIP and SigLIP2; the trained track is an end-to-end Qwen3-VL-4B + LoRA agent that outputs the verification decision and the next action in one forward pass.
    • Built a CoT dataset and post-trained the agent. Generated about 22K CoT samples from benchmark ground truth and per-step visibility metadata, then compared DPO, GRPO and GSPO on top of SFT with a reward combining verification correctness, action quality and format. Accuracy rose from 70.6% untuned to 84.8% with SFT and 85.6% with SFT+GSPO (88.9% with ground-truth boxes), and adding either GRPO or GSPO on top of SFT cut navigation failures from 18.2% to about 9%.
